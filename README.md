### f2 — positions
<img width="959" height="236" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-04 121335" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8b11e210-4b72-4c44-b16c-6c6c3083a6f3" />


Provides a detailed overview of all currently open positions. Tracks entry price, current price, position size, unrealized P&L, stop loss, take profit, risk, and position status.

### f3 — orders

Centralized order management view for monitoring trading orders and their associated execution and position information. Provides visibility into the current order state and trading activity.

### f4 — history

Displays completed trades and historical trading performance. Includes trade timestamps, symbols, sides, entry and exit prices, P&L, trade outcomes, win and loss statistics, and overall performance.

### f5 — scanner

Automated market scanner that evaluates supported crypto and stocks using multiple technical indicators. Ranks assets by signal strength and identifies potential LONG, SHORT, or WAIT opportunities.

### f6 — world map
<img width="959" height="380" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-04 121422" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1f8d0701-d744-4523-8efd-b0d3ce21d294" />



Interactive global market visualization showing market conditions across different countries. Provides country-specific index data, prices, commodities, and relevant financial market information.

### f7 — feeds

System and data feed monitoring dashboard showing the health of market data, OHLCV updates, signal processing, risk management, state saving, web services, and background application processes.

### f8 — risk

Dedicated risk monitoring dashboard for tracking portfolio exposure and capital allocation. Displays risk per trade, deployed capital, reserved capital, stop loss levels, take profit levels, and trailing risk parameters.

### f9 — charts

Interactive market charting interface for visualizing asset price movements and analyzing market trends. Provides graphical price data to support technical and trading analysis.

### f10 — news

Financial news monitoring dashboard that collects and displays market-related headlines and updates. Covers major assets, companies, economic events, macroeconomic developments, and relevant global market news.
